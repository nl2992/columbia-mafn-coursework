# STAT5264-2 — Stochastic Processes

**Term:** Fall 2025  
**Archive type:** Canvas Files export

## Overview

A continuous-time stochastic-processes sequence covering discrete random walks, Brownian motion, stochastic integrals, Itô's lemma, stochastic differential equations, Black–Scholes, equivalent changes of measure, and Girsanov's theorem.

## Module map

- **Parts 1–6:** lecture notes from discrete random walks through change of measure
- **Exam/**: first- and second-half exam problems and retained solutions
- **HW/**: Homework 1–4 and selected solutions
- Additional probability/statistics review and finance applications

Files marked `Ver 1`, `Ver 2`, or `Ver 3` are retained as separate source variants.
