# STAT5264-2 — Stochastic Processes

**Term:** Fall 2025  
**Archive type:** Canvas Files export

## Overview

A continuous-time stochastic-processes sequence covering discrete random walks, Brownian motion, stochastic integrals, Itô's lemma, stochastic differential equations, Black–Scholes, equivalent changes of measure, and Girsanov's theorem.

- [`lectures/`](lectures/): Parts 1–6, Bachelier model variants, and the continuous-time sequence from discrete random walks through change of measure
- [`assignments/`](assignments/): Homework 1–4

- [`assessments/`](assessments/): diagnostic exam and first/second-half exam problems
