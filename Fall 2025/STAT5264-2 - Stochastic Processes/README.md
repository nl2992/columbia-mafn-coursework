**Term:** Fall 2025

## Course focus

This archive is organized around the continuous-time stochastic-processes sequence used in mathematical finance: discrete random walks, Brownian motion, stochastic integrals, Itô’s lemma, stochastic differential equations, Black–Scholes, equivalent changes of measure, and Girsanov’s theorem.
