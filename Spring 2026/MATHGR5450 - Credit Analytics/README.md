**Term:** Spring 2026  
**Archive type:** Canvas Files export

## Course focus

The syllabus describes technical foundations for credit risk, credit derivatives, and counterparty credit risk management. Topics include survival analysis, hazard-rate models, copulas, credit-portfolio loss distributions, credit curves, CDS, CDOs, risky counterparties, exposure modelling, CVA, and machine-learning applications to alternative credit data.
